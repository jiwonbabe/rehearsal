## Thread (5 posts)

1/5
```
Here's the trick: the AI is not allowed to write a single number.

Muse Spark proposes directions, ranges and the bad months. A deterministic Go engine runs the plans. Same input, same output, every time.

If the model quotes a number the engine didn't produce, the sentence is thrown away.
```

2/5
```
The adversary is also an AI, and its only job is to ruin your month.

20 bad months per direction: reach drops 40%, half your creators never post, a competitor runs a 20% sale, ad cost per result up 60%, the holiday lift never comes.

4 direction families × 60 variants = 240 plans, each run against all of them.
```

3/5
```
The verdict isn't "this plan is good." It's "this plan survived 15 of 20 bad months, dies if ad costs rise and reach drops, and here is the ONE assumption that flips the ranking."

For the demo brand: creators posting at 90% instead of 60%. Check that before you spend.
```

4/5
```
It also checks itself before it checks you.

It hides 13 weeks of the brand's own history and tries to reproduce them: typical miss 14.7%, printed on screen.
Levers with no history are marked "not rated" instead of guessed.
The AI's channel picks get checked against Pew's platform data.
```

5/5

What's synthetic: the three demo brands (jewelry, apparel, skincare).
